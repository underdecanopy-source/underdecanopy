## 📱 Component Overview

### ContactBar
- **Purpose:** Sticky mobile contact bar
- **Features:** Phone, WhatsApp, Email buttons + floating WhatsApp
- **Mobile:** Fixed at bottom
- **Desktop:** Floating widgets on right

### ContactSection
- **Purpose:** Comprehensive contact information
- **Features:** Contact cards, address, business hours, Google Maps
- **Responsive:** Single column mobile, multi-column desktop

### SocialLinks
- **Purpose:** Consistent social media links
- **Features:** 3 variants, 3 sizes, lucide-react icons
- **Customizable:** Easy to add/remove social platforms

### MobileOptimizedFooter
- **Purpose:** Mobile-first footer
- **Features:** Quick contact buttons, info, hours, social links
- **Responsive:** Optimized for all screen sizes

### Contact Configuration
- **Purpose:** Centralized contact management
- **Features:** Phone, email, WhatsApp, address, hours, social handles
- **Helper Functions:** getWhatsAppLink(), getPhoneLink(), getEmailLink()
