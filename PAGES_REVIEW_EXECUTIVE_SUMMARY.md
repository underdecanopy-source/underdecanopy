## 🎯 Critical Improvements Needed

### Priority 1: Add Testimonials (CRITICAL)
**Impact:** HIGH | **Effort:** MEDIUM | **Time:** 2-3 hours

**Why:** Builds trust and credibility. Users want to see success stories.

**What to add:**
- 3-4 testimonials per page
- Include name, role, photo, rating
- Real quotes from actual users
- Specific results/benefits mentioned

**Expected Impact:** +1.5 points on overall score

---

### Priority 2: Add Pricing (CRITICAL)
**Impact:** HIGH | **Effort:** LOW | **Time:** 1-2 hours

**Why:** Users need to know costs before engaging.

**What to add:**
- Clear pricing tiers
- Feature comparison
- "What's included" details
- Money-back guarantee (if applicable)

**Expected Impact:** +1 point on overall score

---

### Priority 3: Add Trust Indicators (CRITICAL)
**Impact:** HIGH | **Effort:** LOW | **Time:** 1 hour

**What to add:**
- User statistics (e.g., "50,000+ users")
- Success rates (e.g., "95% success")
- Certifications/partnerships
- Security badges
- Response time guarantees

**Expected Impact:** +1 point on overall score
